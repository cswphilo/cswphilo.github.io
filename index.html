Cheng-Syuan Wan

Researcher in structural proof theory, substructural logic, and formalization of mathematics

I am a researcher in the Logic and Semantics Group at Tallinn University of Technology.

I was a PhD student in the same group during January 2021 to June 2025. My supervisors were Tarmo Uustalu and Niccolò Veltri.

Research Interests

Structural proof theory, substructural logic, categorical and algebraic semantics, formalization of mathematics.
